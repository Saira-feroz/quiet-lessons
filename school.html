A Letter, Long Overdue

✦ ✦ ✦

Dear Sir Abdullah,

There are teachers who instruct, and there are teachers who quietly change the shape of the people around them without ever raising their voice. You have always been the second kind. In the years I have had the privilege of working beside you, I have learned far more from watching you than from any staff meeting or manual.

You have never needed to remind anyone of your experience. You simply carry it — in the way you listen before you speak, the way you forgive a difficult day, the way you stay standing long after everyone else has decided a problem is too hard to solve. I have taken notes, even when you didn't know I was taking them.

This letter is not an evaluation, and it is certainly not a compliment you asked for. It is simply a thank you, written by someone who is a better teacher — and I believe a better person — for having worked beside you.

With respect and gratitude, Your Junior Colleague

What You Have Taught Us

Lessons Beyond the Syllabus

Five things I have learned simply by watching you work.

✦ ✦

Patience

You have never once made a struggling student feel small for being slow. I have watched you explain the same idea five different ways, five different times, without your voice ever losing its warmth.

✦ ✦

Respect

You greet the newest student and the oldest headmaster with the very same courtesy. In your hands, respect was never a performance for those above you — it was simply how you treated everyone.

✦ ✦

Composure

In the loudest staff-room arguments, you are the quiet in the room. I have learned that silence, chosen deliberately, often says more — and settles more — than the loudest defense ever could.

✦ ✦

Loyalty

Through difficult years and easy ones, you never once spoke of this school as anything less than yours to protect. That kind of loyalty cannot be assigned — it has to be chosen, again and again.

✦ ✦

Hard Work

Your lights were on before the gates opened and long after they closed. You never asked to be noticed for it. I noticed anyway.

✦ ✦ ✦ ✦
